Privacy Policy — YouTube Study Notes

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

YouTube Study Notes is a browser extension that helps you create study notes from YouTube video captions. We do not operate a backend server for note generation.

Data we process

  • Video metadata (title, channel, video ID) from pages you open on YouTube.
  • Transcripts from YouTube’s public caption tracks when you generate notes.
  • API keys you enter in Settings, stored in Chrome sync/local storage on your device.
  • Generated notes saved locally in your browser unless you export them.

Third-party services

When you generate notes, transcript text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you select (OpenAI or Google Gemini), using your own API key. We do not receive or store that data on our servers.

What we do not collect

We do not collect account information, analytics, or personal identifiers. We do not sell data.

Permissions

The extension requests access to youtube.com to read captions and show the notes panel, and to OpenAI/Gemini APIs only when you generate notes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing support link.
